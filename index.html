Frequently asked questions

What exactly is permanent lighting?

It's a weatherproof lighting channel installed once along your roofline or trim and left in place year-round, rather than strung up and taken down for each holiday. It stays out of sight during the day and switches on to whatever color or pattern suits the occasion — from a quiet warm white on a Tuesday to a full holiday display in December.

How much does an installation cost?

It depends on the length of your roofline, the layout of your home, and the design you choose. Rather than quote a generic number that may not apply to your property, we'll walk your home and give you a firm estimate as part of your free consultation.

Do I need to supply my own lights?

No — we provide the fixtures as part of the install, using commercial-grade materials chosen to hold up for years. Once installed, the system is yours.

What happens during the design consultation?

We'll walk your property together, talk through your goals and budget, and put together a design and estimate specific to your home. There's no obligation to move forward afterward.

How disruptive is installation, and what about maintenance later?

Installation happens entirely outside your home, so you don't need to rearrange your schedule around it. Afterward, if anything needs attention, you can reach us directly rather than navigating a call center.

How do I change the colors or patterns?

Everything is controlled from an app on your phone. You can switch colors instantly, save presets for holidays or game days, and set schedules so your lights turn on and off automatically at sunset and sunrise.

Do you work with businesses as well as homeowners?

Yes. We install for both residential properties and commercial buildings across our service area.

How long does the lighting typically last?

Well-installed, well-maintained permanent lighting is built to perform for years, not seasons. The biggest factors are fixture quality and proper installation — both of which we control closely, since we're the ones who'll be standing behind the work.
